About the assistant

CUBY is your desktop AI control center.

CUBY listens, speaks, opens apps, checks live information, and connects your personal workflow through MCP-style tools. It is built for fast local use with English and Tamil support, proactive alerts, and a clear human-confirmation boundary for sensitive actions.

What CUBY can do now

These pages reflect the latest assistant features added to this build.

Voice and language

Starts in English with female voice by default. You can switch to Tamil, back to English, and choose male or female voice by command.

English Tamil Male/Female voice

Daily briefing

Combines weather, rain timing, calendar events, reminders, and Gmail interview or assessment updates into one fast summary.

Weather Calendar Gmail

Local controls

Opens installed apps, manages media playback, launches Spotify or YouTube, checks CPU and battery, and handles common desktop tasks.

Apps Spotify YouTube

Live information

Gets dynamic news, weather forecast, train pages, flight data when configured, and smart rain or heat advice for Trichy or other cities.

News Forecast Train info

Utility alerts

Checks power-cut and water-cut notices for Ponnagar, Karumandapam, and Trichy using official pages plus public fallback feeds.

TNPDCL Trichy Corporation Water supply

Guided job search

Opens Naukri, LinkedIn, or Indeed searches for roles such as Gen AI and Python Developer. Final applications remain user-controlled.

Naukri Python Gen AI

Automation with confirmation where it matters

Automatic checks

Weather alerts, due reminders, calendar notifications, Gmail interview scans, public news, and utility fallback checks can run in the background.

Human-confirmed actions

Captcha entry, final job application submission, payment, booking, and sensitive account actions require you to confirm or complete the step.

TNPDCL note: the official outage page uses captcha. CUBY can open the page, guide the check, wait for you to enter captcha, and then read the submitted result.
