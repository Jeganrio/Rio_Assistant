Best daily flow

  1. 1

    Start the assistant

    Click Start AI or say Hey CUBY. CUBY starts in English with female voice.

  2. 2

    Choose voice and language

    Say switch to Tamil, switch to English, switch to male voice, or switch to female voice.

    Ask for the briefing

    Say anything important today to collect weather, rain advice, reminders, calendar, and Gmail updates quickly.

Tamil and Tanglish

Supported Tamil-style examples

Utility alerts

minthadai ponnagar irukka, thanneer varatha karumandapam, and similar Tamil/Tanglish commands are normalized to power and water alert checks.

News and weather

Commands such as top 5 tamil nadu news or trichy weather tomorrow work through the same dynamic routing as English commands.

Use clear speech, pause between commands, and prefer the typed box for long email or job-search requests.
